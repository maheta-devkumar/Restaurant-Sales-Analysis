# 🍽️ Restaurant Sales Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores restaurant data using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze revenue, order trends, restaurant ratings, pricing categories, and other business-related metrics.

The goal is to transform raw data into an interactive dashboard that makes it easier to understand restaurant performance, identify trends, and explore patterns through data visualization.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue and order performance.
- Track monthly revenue and order trends.
- Understand the distribution of restaurants across different price ranges.
- Analyze restaurant ratings across performance categories.
- Explore restaurant characteristics and other relevant business metrics.
- Clean and prepare raw data for meaningful analysis.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** — Calculated columns and measures
- **Data Visualization** — KPI cards, line charts, pie charts, and interactive filters

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Revenue
- Total Orders
- Additional business metrics displayed in the dashboard

### Monthly Performance Analysis
- Total revenue by month
- Total orders by month
- Comparison of monthly performance trends

### Restaurant Analysis
- Distribution of restaurants by price range: Budget, Mid Range, and Premium
- Distribution of restaurants by rating category: Poor, Average, Good, and Excellent
- Analysis of additional restaurant attributes represented in the dashboard

### Data Preparation
- Handled missing and null values where appropriate.
- Cleaned and transformed columns using Power Query.
- Created calculated columns and measures using DAX.
- Prepared data for consistent reporting and visualization.

## 💡 Business Questions Explored

This dashboard explores the following business questions:

1. **How does total revenue change month by month?**  
   Identify monthly revenue trends, fluctuations, and periods of decline or growth.

2. **Which cities generate the highest order volume?**  
   Compare the top 10 cities by number of orders to identify high-demand locations.

3. **What is the distribution of order statuses?**  
   Analyze the proportions of delivered, cancelled, and delayed orders to understand order fulfilment performance.

4. **Which restaurants receive the highest number of orders?**  
   Identify the top 10 restaurants by order volume and compare their popularity.

5. **What are the key performance indicators of the business?**  
   Monitor total revenue, total orders, average order value, and average delivery time in one place.
